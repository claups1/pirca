Pirca

The problem

Small shop owners in Piura sell from ground floors, keep stock near the floor, depend on suppliers in other cities, and have little cash. El Niño costero floods their shops and cuts their roads.

Four agents, one wall

  • ManagerRuns the team and writes the final plan.
  • Risk AnalystReads ENFEN alerts and Piura's flood history.
  • PlannerSizes stock, cash and suppliers; picks purchases within budget.
  • CriticStress-tests the plan; can send it back once.
Numbers come from code, not the model.

All agents: gpt-6-luna via Portkey, low reasoning effort.

Limits and next steps

Honest limits
  • 8 of 15 protection factors are illustrative estimates.
  • The Critic asks for a revision on every run (6 of 6).
  • One real user so far (from Lima), and not hosted: it runs locally.
Next steps
  • WhatsApp access: answer and receive the plan there.
  • District view for a municipality: where help is needed.
  • Live ENFEN updates: tell owners when the alert changes.
  • Landlords of commercial premises (requested by a user).
